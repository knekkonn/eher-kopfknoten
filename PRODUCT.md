# ABGRUND

ABGRUND is a German-first knowledge game. Players answer seven open questions, with 30 seconds per question. Less common, valid answers earn more points. A run reaches at most 700 points / 7,000 metres; cosmetic characters unlock from the best single run.

The current product includes guest play, optional Firebase accounts, a personal history, private friend profiles, answer reports and curated daily review. The existing underwater pixel scene and six SVG characters are product assets to preserve.

This brief is derived from the existing game and the owner's requested improvements on 4–7 November 2026. This update refines the existing experience; it does not introduce a new brand or change the scoring model.

Priorities:

- Start a round quickly, on a phone, iPad or desktop.
- Make the question, answer and time remaining easy to read.
- Present a verified German equivalent first; keep English accepted with identical points. Keep brands and ambiguous names intact.
- Keep reports, animations and temporary network failures from blocking play.
- Share statistics only between mutually confirmed friends.
- Show accurate loading, error and empty states; do not claim deployments or verification without evidence.

Success is assessed through answer-identity/points checks, existing game and Firestore tests, keyboard/modal checks and browser views at 320, 390, 820, 1024 and 1440 pixels. Live deployment is a separate verification step.
